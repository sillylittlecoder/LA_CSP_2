St. George: Utah's Dixie

Whether it's a staycation, or you're coming from out of state, St. George is the place to visit! Known for its palm trees, red rocks, and warm weather; Utah's Dixie makes it the perfect place for explorers to find their next challenge that may turn into a yearly regular. Here, you'll find some awesome ideas on planning a trip to St. George.

You may be wondering why you should come...and it's because there are endless possibilities of the adventures you could take! Here's some of my top recommendations:

  • Dune Driving/Off-roading:Adventerous off-roading with full desert vibes
  • Zion Canyon Hot Springs:One of the most top rated resorts! full of St. George's natural hotsprings, sauna, cold plunges, and recreated hot springs from around the world including places from india and japan
  • Canyon Hiking: There are endless spots to go hiking! You can choose the colorful Yellow Mountain, The Narrows, or endless trails up Zion's National Park
  • Zion's National Park: This is the most colorful place around St. George and it has endless adventures inside itself such as stargazing, river-rafting, and biking
