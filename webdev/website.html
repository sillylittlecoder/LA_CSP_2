CrossFit Explained

Let's start out with a definition of CrossFit: CrossFit is a form of high-intensity interval training that consists of Olympic weightlifting, powerlifting, gymnastics, and metabolic conditioning(cardio), including rowing, running, and swimming. In simple terms, it is a form of working out that consists of multiple sports that are often combined in variations into one workout. CrossFit is a relatively new sport. It was created in the 1980's by Greg Glassman, who was a professional gymnast that spent 30 years constructing a form of working out before ever revealing it to the world. On February 10th in 2001, the first CrossFit workout was posted. After only three years(2004) of being introduced, CrossFit became the fastest-growing business every, reaching 15,000 affiliates in only 15 years. However, soon after this (in 2013) the NSCA released a study that claimed CrossFit contained a high risk for injury and essentially they wanted to end Greg Glassman's business. Thankfully, CrossFit countered these claims and was able to continue in its growth. Today, there is an estimate of over 4 million people who take part in CrossFit and it is practiced in 150 countries!
